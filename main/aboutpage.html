A job board built for both sides of the hire.

WorkZone connects job seekers with employers. Seekers find openings and apply in a few clicks; employers post jobs and review applicants without spreadsheets or email chains.

Every application has a clear status — pending, approved, accepted, declined or rejected — so nobody is left guessing.

For job seekers

  • Search and filter open jobs
  • Save jobs to look at later
  • Apply and follow your application status
  • Accept or decline an offer

For employers

  • Post job openings
  • See who applied, with their skills
  • Approve or reject applicants
